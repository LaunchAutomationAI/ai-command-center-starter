## Expected Topics Over Time

These topics will appear here as you start putting content into them. Claude creates each one the first time you have something real to file there.

- `operations/` - SOPs, processes, internal systems
- `sales-and-demos/` - sales scripts, demo flows, objection handling
- `marketing/` - campaigns, hooks, channels, results
- `products/` - product line, pricing, packaging
- `ai-agents/` - custom AI personas and workflows
- `partnerships/` - affiliates, referrals, joint ventures
- `industry/` - industry knowledge, vendors, regulations
- `competitors/` - competitive intel
- `voice-and-copy/` - brand voice, hooks, copy library
- `personal/` - finances, health, personal projects (optional)

Topics are created lazily. Do not pre-build empty topic folders. A topic gets created the first time there is real content to put in it.
