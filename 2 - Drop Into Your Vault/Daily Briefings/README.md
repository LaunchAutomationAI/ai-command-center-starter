# Daily Briefings

Auto-generated daily summaries of your voice captures.

This folder stays empty until both the voice capture daemon and the daily briefing summarizer are set up by your coach (usually after the foundation is in place).

Once active, every morning a new file appears for the previous day:

`2026-06-10.md`

Inside, a structured briefing:

- **TL;DR** - one-paragraph summary of yesterday
- **Key Decisions** - decisions you made
- **Action Items** - what you committed to
- **Client Mentions** - which clients came up, in what context
- **Things to Remember** - quirks, ideas, reminders worth keeping

Cost is low (under $1/month at typical use), summarization runs overnight, and the files live entirely in your vault.

Skim yesterday's briefing every morning. Most useful five minutes of the day.
